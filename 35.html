overflow: visible (default)

This text is intentionally long enough to overflow past the box's fixed height, and with visible, it just spills out past the border without being clipped or scrolled.

overflow: hidden

This text is intentionally long enough to overflow past the box's fixed height, but with hidden, anything past the edge is simply cut off and invisible.

overflow: scroll

This text is intentionally long enough to overflow past the box's fixed height, so with scroll, a scrollbar appears — and it would appear even if the text were short enough to fit.

overflow: auto

This text is intentionally long enough to overflow past the box's fixed height, so with auto, a scrollbar appears only because it's actually needed here.
